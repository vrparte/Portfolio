# Varad Parte Portfolio

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and engineering collaborators assessing Varad for Applied AI, LLM engineering, and software roles. They need to understand his current focus, experience, and technical work quickly on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose
Replace the outdated portfolio at varadparte.com with a personal, independently hosted version of the current résumé content. Success means a visitor can assess the work, inspect verified project repositories, and contact Varad directly.

## Brand Personality
Clear, technical, grounded. A polished personal identity with the readability of the supplied temporary portfolio.

## Anti-references
Third-party advertising, sponsorship, application-service calls to action, invented impact claims, fake demos, skill percentage bars, and the outdated site's generic claims.

## Design Principles
- Lead with the current Applied AI focus.
- Make experience and project substance easy to scan.
- Use only supplied or verified facts and working links.
- Keep ownership and maintenance straightforward through GitHub.
- Prefer a fast, readable document over decorative complexity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks and headings, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, responsive reflow down to 320px, and a printable version. No additional specific needs have been provided.

## Basis
User brief, inspection of the supplied temporary portfolio and existing domain, and verified public repositories. Audience and visual tone are working assumptions pending optional user feedback.
